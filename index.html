What this is

We opened Translate Now the way a new user does, then held its paywall up against 78,000 others.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, and every year we publish what their paywalls do in State of Paywalls. We installed Translate Now, met the paywall on first launch, tapped through it and past it, read your App Store listing, and compared what we saw with the report.

What follows is four ideas, each one an A/B test you could run, each built into a clickable prototype. Benchmarks are for all categories in the US: the report has no Reference category, and the US is Translate Now's biggest App Store market.

We did not redesign your paywall

Everything in the prototype is yours: the black screen, the AIR APPS wordmark, your emoji list, your blue button, and the Air Apps Plans card from your Settings. We added two rows you already own and moved what happens after “Not now”.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If one of these is something you have already run, tell us and we will go deeper on the rest rather than repeat it.
